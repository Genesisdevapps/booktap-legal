# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · BookTap version 1.0

## Short answer

BookTap has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone
number, location, contacts or photos. No ads, no ad networks, and we do
not track you across apps. We do not sell, rent or share your data for
marketing.

The text you read, the words you tap and save, your search queries and
your review notes are never sent to us. The only exception is a sentence
you ask to be read aloud, which is handed to Apple's own text-to-speech
service on the device, as described in section 5.

Three things do leave the device: usage events that identify no one and
show which steps of the app work, a technical crash record if the app
crashes, and whether your subscription is valid. You can switch off the
first two under Settings > Data.

## 2 · Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in your device's own memory and is not sent
to us: your reading progress, saved words, tapped word stems, review
notes, level-test result, native and learning language choice, reading
preferences such as type size and theme, daily goal, sound and
notification settings.

If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it. Settings > Data >
"Reset data" does the same without deleting the App. Because we hold no
backup of this data we cannot restore it once deleted; your device's own
backup system (such as iCloud backup) is subject to Apple's rules.

## 3 · Data that leaves the device

There are three items and we describe each one separately. Each
processor acts on our instructions under a data processing agreement,
applies at least the level of protection described here and may not use
the data for its own purposes.

## 3.1 · Usage measurement

What is sent: 27 events from a closed list predefined inside the app —
finishing the introduction, opening a book, tapping a word, the
subscription screen appearing, and so on. Events carry only values from
closed sets: book identifier, language code, plan name, level,
subscription status and count buckets; plus the app version, operating
system version and device model.

What is not sent: the word itself, your search query, sentence text,
your name, e-mail, location or advertising identifier. Free text is
never sent.

Identity: a random identifier generated by the measurement tool itself
is used, and we hold no record linking that identifier to you. No person
profile is created and your IP address is not stored.

Processor: PostHog (PostHog Inc.), European Union cloud (Frankfurt,
Germany). Data is stored in the EU; if PostHog personnel outside the
EU/EEA access it for support, that access relies on standard contractual
clauses. Legal basis: legitimate interest (KVKK art. 5/2-f · GDPR art.
6/1-f) — we cannot improve the product without measuring it; the balance
of interests is protected by the anonymous, closed-list design.
Objection: the "Usage measurement" switch under Settings > Data. Sending
stops the moment you switch it off. Retention: at most 12 months;
afterwards deleted or kept only as aggregate statistics.

## 3.2 · Crash reports

What is sent: when the app crashes or an error is caught, the technical
stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version and a
random per-installation identifier.

Processor: Google LLC (Firebase Crashlytics). Data is transferred to the
United States; the transfer relies on Google's standard contractual
clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, with the safeguards of
KVKK art. 9. Firebase Analytics is deliberately disabled. Legal basis:
legitimate interest (KVKK art. 5/2-f · GDPR art. 6/1-f) — an app that
does not crash is a product requirement. Objection: the "Crash reports"
switch under Settings > Data. Retention: 90 days.

The Settings > Diagnostics screen shows you the last lines of the same
records; that log never leaves your device and is kept even if you
switch the setting off.

## 3.3 · Subscription

What is sent: whether your subscription is valid, the identifier of the
purchased product, store country, app version, the transaction
identifier issued by the App Store and a random per-subscriber
identifier. We never see your card details, your name or your Apple
account.

Processors: Adapty (subscription infrastructure) and Apple (App Store /
StoreKit). Collection of the advertising identifier (IDFA) and IP
address in Adapty has been disabled for this app. Where Adapty processes
data outside the EU/EEA and Türkiye, the transfer relies on the
safeguards of GDPR Chapter V and KVKK art. 9 (standard contractual
clauses). Legal basis: performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c · GDPR
art. 6/1-b) — knowing whether to unlock is the subscription itself.
Objection: this item has no off switch and cannot have one; without a
subscription the first book still opens in full, free of charge.
Retention: for the duration of the subscription relationship and
afterwards for the accounting and limitation periods (at most 10 years).

## 4 · Advertising and tracking

We show no ads. We do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), which
is why iOS's tracking permission prompt never appears. We do not sell,
rent or transfer your data to any ad network, data broker or other app.
There is no profiling and no decision affecting you that is based solely
on automated processing.

## 5 · System services

- Read-aloud uses the operating system's own text-to-speech engine.
  The sentence being read is passed to the operating system; how
  Apple processes that data is subject to Apple's own privacy policy.
- Notifications (word review and continue-reading reminders) are
  scheduled locally on the device; their text and times never leave
  the device. You can switch them off under Settings > Notifications.
- The App connects to the internet only for the three items in
  section 3; connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

## 6 · Security

Data on your device is stored in the operating system's app sandbox and
cannot be read by other apps. Data that leaves the device is sent over
encrypted connections and only to the processors named above. No system
can guarantee absolute security; if we learn of a breach affecting your
personal data we will make the notifications required by applicable law.

## 7 · Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 and GDPR art. 15-22 you have the right to access,
rectify and erase your data, to request restriction of processing, to
data portability and to object to processing. You may object at any time
to processing based on legitimate interest (measurement and crash
reports); the fastest way to object is the switch under Settings > Data.

A word of honesty: measurement and crash records carry no name or
contact detail and are keyed only to a random identifier we cannot link
to you. Because we hold no record that identifies you, we may be unable
to confirm that a given record is yours; in that case we do not ask you
for additional identifying information and may be unable to fulfil the
request (GDPR art. 11). The switch is always in your hands.

Deletion: the data in section 2 is deleted when you delete the App or
use Settings > Data > "Reset data". For the items in section 3, write to
us; usage and crash records also expire on their own under the retention
periods above, and subscription records are deleted when their retention
period ends.

Requests: genesisdevapps@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days at the
latest. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish
Personal Data Protection Board (kvkk.gov.tr) or with the data protection
authority of the EU country of your habitual residence (for Italy, the
Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

## 8 · Children

The App is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect
personal data from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13
has sent us data, we delete it. If you believe a child in your care is
using the App, write to us.

## 9 · International transfers

Usage measurement data stays within the EU (Frankfurt). Crash reports
may be transferred to the United States and subscription data to
wherever Adapty processes it; every transfer relies on the safeguards
stated in section 3. For users in Türkiye, transfers abroad are made
within the framework of KVKK art. 9.

## 10 · Changes

If this policy changes we publish the current version inside the App and
on the store page, with the date of change at the top, and we announce
any material change in an App update. If the App's data behaviour
changes, this text changes in the same update: the label and the
behaviour never diverging is a rule for us. The App's privacy label on
the App Store is kept consistent with this policy.
