# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · BookTap version 1.1

## Short answer

BookTap has no accounts. We do not ask for your real name, e-mail,
phone number, location or contacts, and the App does not read your
photos. The only name the App asks for is a nickname for your traveler
character, and it stays on your device. No ads, no ad networks, and we
do not track you across apps. We do not sell, rent or share your data
for marketing.

The text you read, the words you tap and save, your search queries,
your review notes and your individual answers in lessons and games
are never sent to us. The only exception is a sentence you ask to be
read aloud, which is handed to Apple's own text-to-speech service on
the device, as described in section 5.

Three things do leave the device: pseudonymous usage events from a
closed list (section 3.1), a technical crash record if the App crashes
(section 3.2), and whether your subscription is valid (section 3.3).
You can switch off the first two at any time under Settings > Data.

## 2 · Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in your device's own memory and is not sent
to us:

- Reading: your reading progress, saved words, tapped word stems, review
  notes, level-test result, native and learning language, and reading
  preferences such as type size, theme and sounds.
- Learning and games: your progress through the grammar lessons and
  exercises, your streak, streak freezes and repairs, the badges you
  earn, your daily goal and daily activity log, the weekly summary, and
  your word race scores, personal records and the list of words the
  race has already shown you.
- Free tier: which books and grammar topics are open to you free of
  charge, the days you opened the App for the gift books, and the days
  you used your free word races (Terms of Use, section 6).
- Your traveler: the nickname and the look you choose (skin tone, hair,
  clothes, glasses, background).
- Notification settings and the times you chose.

The share card (a picture of your streak or weekly summary, without your
nickname) is drawn on the device. It leaves the App only when you tap
"Share": it is then handed to the iOS share sheet, and you decide where
it goes. If you choose to save it to Photos, iOS asks for permission to
add a photo; the App cannot read your photo library.

If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it. Settings > Data >
"Reset data" does the same without deleting the App. Because we hold no
copy of this data we cannot restore it once deleted; your device's own
backup system (such as iCloud backup) is subject to Apple's rules.

## 3 · Data that leaves the device

There are three items and we describe each one separately. Each
processor named below acts only on our instructions under a data
processing agreement, must apply at least the level of protection
described here and may not use the data for its own purposes.

## 3.1 · Usage measurement

Purpose: to learn which parts of the App work and which do not (for
example, where people stop in the introduction, whether a feature is
used at all, whether a screen fails), so that we can fix and improve
the App. We do not use these events to build a picture of you.

What is sent: 62 kinds of event from a closed list fixed inside the
App's code. Nothing outside this list is sent, and the list can only
grow in an App update together with this policy. The events fall into
these groups:

- Getting started: steps of the introduction, the pages of the short
  "what's new" tour shown once after an update and whether it was
  finished or skipped, the first opening of the App on a given day (at
  most once a day), the result of the notification permission request
  and the screen it was asked from,
  the level test being started and finished, and the language pair
  being changed in settings.
- Reading and words: a book being opened, progressing, finished or
  failing to open; a word being tapped or saved (never the word
  itself); the sentence translation card being opened by a long press;
  read-aloud being started and finished; word review sessions; that a
  search was used and which kind of result was opened (book, word,
  grammar or text), never the search query.
- Grammar: a lesson topic being opened, a part, exercise set or exam
  being finished, a topic being sealed and a grammar level (A1 to C1)
  being completed.
- Motivation features: the streak being extended, a streak freeze or
  repair being used, the streak-in-danger and streak-broken screens
  being shown, a badge being earned, the daily goal being set or
  reached (at most once a day), the weekly summary being viewed, the
  share button being tapped, and gift books being granted or finished.
- Word race: a game being started, finished or quit midway, with the
  difficulty band (easy, medium, hard), the chosen game length in
  seconds, the number of correct and total answers, whether a new
  personal record was set, and whether the free daily game or Pro was
  used.
- Navigation and settings: which tab or which screen from a fixed list
  was viewed (for example the badge album, wardrobe, progress, all
  books, a book page, a word page, settings, reminders, daily goal,
  word race setup, the grammar path and contents, the roadmap); which
  setting from a fixed list was changed and, for on/off or multiple
  choice settings, the option chosen; a reminder being switched on or
  off; which type of notification opened the App (word review,
  reminder, keep-reading, streak, weekly summary, gift book, word
  race); the end of a session, with its length only as a range (under
  1 minute, 1-3, 3-10, 10-30 or more than 30 minutes); "Reset data"
  being used; and the system rating prompt being requested.
- Subscription screen: the screen being shown or closed and where it
  was opened from, which version of it was shown, a plan being
  selected, a purchase or free trial being started, completed or
  failing, the thank-you screen shown after a purchase or free trial
  starts, a restore attempt and its result, and technical failures
  (such as prices not loading).

Each event carries only values from closed sets: a book identifier, a
language code, a plan name, a screen or setting name from a fixed list,
a level code, yes/no values, counts of answers in a game, a percentage
of progress, and ranges instead of exact numbers (for example, "days
since installation" is sent only as day 0, day 1, days 2-7, days 8-30
or more than 30). Every event also carries the same small context: the
language you are learning, your native language, whether you have Pro,
and your reading level (A1 to C1, or not set); plus the app version,
operating system version and device model.

What is never sent: the word itself, your search query, the text of any
sentence, your individual answers, your nickname, name, e-mail,
location, contacts or the advertising identifier (IDFA). Free text you
type is never sent. Your IP address is necessarily seen when the
connection is made, but it is not stored with the events.

Identity: the measurement tool generates its own random identifier on
your device. We do not call any "identify" function, we create no
person profile, and we hold no record linking that identifier to you.
Because events from the same installation share that identifier, the
data is pseudonymous rather than anonymous, and we treat it as personal
data.

Processor: PostHog (PostHog Inc.), European Union cloud (Frankfurt,
Germany). Data is stored in the EU; if PostHog personnel outside the
EU/EEA access it for support, that access relies on standard contractual
clauses. The measurement tool's own automatic events (app lifecycle,
rage clicks, surveys, feature flags, push notification events) are
switched off: only the events on the closed list are sent.

Legal basis: legitimate interest (KVKK art. 5/2-f · GDPR art. 6/1-f).
Our interest is to understand and improve a small product that has no
other source of feedback. We weighed it against your interests: the
events contain no content and no contact details (each event carries
only the time it occurred, and session length is sent only as a range);
they are tied only to a random identifier; no profile is built
and no decision about you is made from them; they are stored in the EU
for a limited time; and you can stop them with one switch. We consider
that this does not override your interests, rights and freedoms.

Your right to object: use the "Usage measurement" switch under
Settings > Data. Sending stops the moment you switch it off; you do not
need to give a reason. Retention: at most 12 months; afterwards deleted
or kept only as aggregate statistics that no longer relate to an
identifier.

## 3.2 · Crash reports

What is sent: when the App crashes or an error is caught, the technical
stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version and a
random per-installation identifier.

Processor: Google LLC (Firebase Crashlytics). Data is transferred to the
United States; the transfer relies on Google's standard contractual
clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and for users in Türkiye
on the safeguards of KVKK art. 9. Firebase Analytics is deliberately
disabled. Legal basis: legitimate interest (KVKK art. 5/2-f · GDPR art.
6/1-f): an App that does not crash is a basic product requirement, and
the report contains no content you created. Objection: the "Crash
reports" switch under Settings > Data. Retention: 90 days.

The Settings > Diagnostics screen shows you the last lines of the same
records; that log never leaves your device and is kept even if you
switch the setting off.

## 3.3 · Subscription

What is sent: whether your subscription is valid, the identifier of the
purchased product, store country, app version, the transaction
identifier issued by the App Store, a random per-installation
identifier, device model and operating system version, language and
region setting, time zone, and Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV).
We never see your card details, your name or your Apple account.

About the IDFV: it is a device identifier that Apple gives to apps from
the same developer; it is not the advertising identifier (IDFA), it
cannot be used to recognise you in other developers' apps and it is not
used for tracking. It is sent to Adapty, together with the identifiers
above, only for operating your subscription (App Functionality) and is
not linked to your identity.

Processor: Adapty (subscription infrastructure). Collection of the
advertising identifier (IDFA) and of the IP address in Adapty has been
disabled for this App. Where Adapty processes data outside the EU/EEA
and Türkiye, the transfer relies on the safeguards of GDPR Chapter V
and KVKK art. 9 (standard contractual clauses). Apple processes the
purchase itself (App Store / StoreKit) as an independent controller.

Legal basis: performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c · GDPR art.
6/1-b): checking whether to unlock Pro is the subscription itself; the
records kept afterwards rely on our legal obligations (KVKK art. 5/2-ç
· GDPR art. 6/1-c) and on the establishment or defence of legal claims
(KVKK art. 5/2-e). This item has no off switch and cannot have one;
without a subscription the free tier described in the Terms of Use
stays available. Retention: for the duration of the subscription
relationship and afterwards for the applicable accounting and
limitation periods (at most 10 years).

## 4 · Advertising, tracking and automated decisions

We show no ads. We do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), which
is why iOS's tracking permission prompt never appears. We do not sell,
rent or transfer your data to any ad network, data broker or other app.

There is no profiling and no decision that produces legal effects or
similarly significantly affects you based solely on automated
processing. The level test's level recommendation, the choice of gift
books and of the words in the word race, and the order of your word
reviews are calculated on your device; they only adjust what the App
shows you, and you can retake the level test at any time.

## 5 · System services and notifications

- Read-aloud uses the operating system's own text-to-speech engine.
  The sentence being read is passed to the operating system; how
  Apple processes that data is subject to Apple's own privacy policy.
- All notifications are scheduled locally on the device; we run no
  push notification server. They are: the daily word review reminder;
  the keep-reading reminder (your unfinished book or your waiting
  words); the evening streak-in-danger notice (22:00); the notice the
  evening before a streak milestone (21:00); the weekly summary on Sunday
  evening; the gift book notices (in the evening of the following days
  while gift books are still to come, only if you do not have Pro); and
  the word race notice (in the evening of the day after you played, to
  say a new game is waiting, or for Pro to invite a new record
  attempt; for the free tier it stops once your free race days are
  used).
- The gift book and word race notices belong to the "Keep-reading
  reminder" and stop when you switch it off. After you start a free
  trial, the App sends none of these reminders until you switch a
  reminder on yourself or the trial turns into a paid subscription.
- Notification text (which may include your traveler's nickname) and
  times never leave the device. You can switch them off under
  Settings > Notifications, or all of them at once in iOS Settings.
- The App connects to the internet only for the three items in
  section 3 (and, for purchases, to Apple); connections are encrypted
  (HTTPS/TLS).

## 6 · Retention at a glance

- Data on your device (section 2): until you delete the App or use
  "Reset data".
- Usage measurement (section 3.1): at most 12 months.
- Crash reports (section 3.2): 90 days.
- Subscription records (section 3.3): for the subscription relationship
  and then the accounting and limitation periods, at most 10 years.

We review these periods regularly and delete or anonymise data when its
period ends (KVKK art. 7 · GDPR art. 5/1-e).

## 7 · Security

Data on your device is stored in the operating system's app sandbox and
cannot be read by other apps. Data that leaves the device is sent over
encrypted connections and only to the processors named above. No system
can guarantee absolute security; if we learn of a breach affecting your
personal data we will make the notifications required by applicable law
(KVKK art. 12 · GDPR art. 33-34).

## 8 · Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 you may ask whether your data is processed, request
information about it, learn the purpose and whether it is used for that
purpose, learn the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is
transferred, request rectification or erasure and notification of
these to recipients, object to a result against you arising solely
from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by
unlawful processing. Under GDPR art. 15-22 you have the right of
access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and
objection. You may object at any time to processing based on
legitimate interest (usage measurement and crash reports); the fastest
way is the switch under Settings > Data, which works immediately.

A word of honesty: usage and crash records carry no name or contact
detail and are keyed only to a random identifier we cannot link to
you. Because we hold no record that identifies you, we may be unable to
confirm that a given record is yours; in that case we will tell you, we
do not ask you for additional identifying information, and we may be
unable to fulfil the request (GDPR art. 11). The switches are always in
your hands.

Deletion: the data in section 2 is deleted when you delete the App or
use Settings > Data > "Reset data". For the items in section 3, write to
us; usage and crash records also expire on their own under the
retention periods above, and subscription records are deleted when
their retention period ends.

Requests: write to genesisdevapps@gmail.com. We answer free of charge
and within 30 days at the latest. You also have the right to lodge a
complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority
(kvkk.gov.tr) or with the data protection authority of the EU country
of your habitual residence, place of work or of the alleged
infringement (for Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati
personali, garanteprivacy.it).

## 9 · Children

The App is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, and no
processing described here relies on consent. If we learn that data
from a child under 13 has reached us, we delete it. If you believe a
child in your care is using the App, write to us; you can also switch
off usage measurement and crash reports on the child's device under
Settings > Data.

within the framework of KVKK art. 9.
